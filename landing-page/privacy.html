PRIVACY & AI DATA USE

No smoke. No mirrors. Here’s what the AI sees.

Snippet Bubbles is local-first. Using the AI assistant is optional, but it is a server-assisted feature: the text you submit must leave your device so the built-in AI service can respond.

Keep secrets out of AI prompts. Do not submit passwords, API keys, private credentials, personal information, or confidential material you are not authorized to share. Redact sensitive values before asking the assistant to generate, explain, convert, or improve code.

What the AI receives

For an AI request, the built-in AI service receives the messages needed to answer: your prompt, any code or snippet text you include, and the personality instructions you configure. The service uses that information to generate the requested response. No external AI API key is required from you.

What we retain for reliability and abuse protection

We retainWe do not retain in AI telemetry
Action type, timestamp, request and response character counts, outcome, duration, quota window, and an account-linked or anonymized identifier.Your prompt text, code, snippets, model response text, email address, or raw IP address.

The anonymized identifier is derived with a one-way server-side hash so anonymous requests can be rate-limited without keeping the raw network address in telemetry.

Limits and accounts

AI use is limited per signed-in account with hourly and daily windows. Before sign-in, a smaller limit applies to an anonymized connection identifier. The app displays the current hourly allowance in Settings so you are not left guessing why an AI request was rejected.

Your snippets and cloud backup

Your library works locally by default. If you choose to sign in and use cloud backup or sync, the records needed for that feature are stored in your account’s cloud data. Public sharing creates a revocable, optionally expiring snapshot of the snippet you choose to share.
